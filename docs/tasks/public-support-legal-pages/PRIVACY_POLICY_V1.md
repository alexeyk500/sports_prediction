# Privacy Policy

**Version:** 1.0\
**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Goalstery ("Goalstery", "we", "us", or
"our") handles information when you use the Goalstery Telegram Mini App.

By using Goalstery, you acknowledge the practices described in this
Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Receive

### Telegram Account Information

Goalstery operates as a Telegram Mini App. When you use Goalstery, we
may receive account information made available through the Telegram Mini
App interface that is necessary to identify you and provide the Service.

Depending on the information available from Telegram, this may include:

-   your Telegram user ID;
-   username;
-   first and last name or display name;
-   profile photo or avatar information;
-   language or other account-related information made available through
    the Telegram Mini App interface.

Goalstery does not require a separate username or password.

We do not request access to your Telegram messages, contacts, or phone
number as part of the normal operation of Goalstery.

Telegram is an independent service and processes information under its
own terms and privacy practices. Goalstery does not control how Telegram
processes your information.

## 2. Goalstery Activity Data

We process information generated through your use of Goalstery,
including information such as predictions you submit, prediction
outcomes, Cups and other in-game scoring information, leaderboard
positions, participation in Cups and competitions, match-related
activity, profile preferences and settings, and timestamps or other
information necessary to operate the Service.

This information is used to provide Goalstery's gameplay, scoring,
competition, leaderboard, account, and related functionality.

## 3. Technical Data

Goalstery does not intentionally maintain a separate product analytics
profile based on your IP address, browser, device, or similar technical
identifiers.

However, limited technical information may necessarily be processed
temporarily by servers, network infrastructure, hosting systems,
Cloudflare, Telegram, or other infrastructure required to deliver and
secure the Service.

Such processing may occur for purposes such as routing network traffic,
security, abuse prevention, diagnostics, and reliable operation of the
Service.

## 4. Analytics and Tracking

Goalstery does not currently operate a dedicated behavioral analytics or
user-tracking system.

If this changes materially, this Privacy Policy will be updated as
appropriate.

## 5. Advertising

Goalstery may display advertising.

Goalstery does not currently identify a specific advertising provider in
this Privacy Policy.

If third-party advertising services are introduced, those services may
process certain technical, advertising, or interaction information
according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Before introducing an advertising provider whose processing materially
affects the practices described in this Privacy Policy, Goalstery may
update this Privacy Policy and provide additional information where
appropriate.

## 6. How We Use Information

We use information processed through Goalstery to:

-   provide and operate the Service;
-   authenticate and identify users through Telegram;
-   process predictions and match results;
-   calculate Cups, rankings, and competition results;
-   operate leaderboards and Cups;
-   maintain user preferences;
-   prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, manipulation, and unauthorized
    access;
-   investigate technical problems;
-   maintain the security and integrity of Goalstery;
-   respond to support requests;
-   comply with applicable legal obligations where required.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 7. Infrastructure and Service Providers

Goalstery is operated using its own server infrastructure, including a
privately managed VPS.

Goalstery also uses Cloudflare for infrastructure-related services that
may include network delivery, security, traffic routing, and protection
of the Service.

Goalstery also relies on Telegram as the platform through which the Mini
App is accessed.

Football and match information may be obtained from third-party
sports-data providers, including football-data.org. These providers are
used for sports information and are not intended to receive Goalstery
user profiles merely because you submit a prediction.

Additional infrastructure or service providers may be introduced when
necessary to operate Goalstery. This Privacy Policy will be updated
where a change materially affects the processing of personal information
described here.

## 8. Prize Information

Some Goalstery competitions may offer real-world prizes, including
cryptocurrency prizes such as USDT or GRAM.

Participation in Goalstery does not normally require you to provide a
cryptocurrency wallet address.

If you are eligible to receive a cryptocurrency prize, you may be asked
to contact Goalstery Support and voluntarily provide a wallet address
required to deliver the prize.

Wallet information provided for prize delivery will be used for purposes
related to verifying and processing the applicable prize payment and
handling related support or compliance matters.

You should never provide Goalstery with a private key, seed phrase,
wallet password, or similar wallet credentials. Goalstery does not need
such information to send a prize.

## 9. Data Retention

Goalstery retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to
operate the Service, maintain competition and leaderboard integrity,
prevent abuse, resolve disputes, comply with applicable obligations, and
protect the Service.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and
the reason it is retained.

## 10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Goalstery account and associated
personal information by contacting Goalstery Support through the
official Telegram support account.

When a valid deletion request is processed, Goalstery will delete or
anonymize personal information associated with the account, subject to
information that must reasonably be retained for legal, security,
fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or similar legitimate purposes.

Where historical competition, prediction, or leaderboard information
needs to be preserved to maintain the integrity of completed
competitions or records, Goalstery may retain that information in
anonymized form so that it is no longer associated with your Telegram
identity.

## 11. Age Requirement

Goalstery is intended only for users who are at least 18 years old.

If you are under 18, you must not use Goalstery.

## 12. International Use

Goalstery is intended to be accessible internationally.

Because Goalstery operates globally, information may be processed in
countries other than the country in which you are located.

Where applicable law imposes specific requirements concerning the
processing or transfer of personal information, Goalstery will address
those requirements as applicable to the Service.

## 13. Security

Goalstery uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended
to protect information and maintain the security of the Service.

However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Telegram
account and any cryptocurrency wallet information you choose to provide
for prize delivery.

## 14. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights
concerning your personal information, which may include rights to
request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other actions.

Requests may be submitted through the official Goalstery Telegram
support account.

Goalstery may need to verify that a request relates to the correct
Goalstery account before acting on it.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Goalstery may update this Privacy Policy when the Service, its
technology, its providers, its advertising practices, or applicable
requirements change.

The current version and its Last Updated date will be made available
through Goalstery.

Material changes may be communicated through the Service where
appropriate.

## 16. Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, or other
privacy-related requests, contact Goalstery through its official
Telegram support account.

The specific official support account displayed by Goalstery should be
used to avoid impersonation or fraudulent support accounts.
