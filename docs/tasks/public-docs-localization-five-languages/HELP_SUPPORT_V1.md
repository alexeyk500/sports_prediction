# Help & Support

**Version:** 1.0\
**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026

Welcome to Goalstery Help & Support.

Here you can learn how predictions, Cups, leaderboards, Prize Cups,
accounts, and other Goalstery features work.

If you cannot find the answer you need, contact the Goalstery support
team through the official Telegram support account shown in the app.

## Predictions

### How do predictions work?

Goalstery lets you make predictions about football matches.

Available prediction types may vary depending on the match. Different
prediction types can have different difficulty levels and may award
different amounts of Cups when predicted correctly.

Select an available match, choose your prediction, and submit it before
the prediction deadline.

Once your prediction has been submitted, Goalstery records it and later
evaluates it using the settled match result.

### When does a prediction lock?

Every prediction has a deadline.

Once the applicable deadline has passed or the prediction has otherwise
been locked by Goalstery, you can no longer change that prediction.

Make sure your selection is correct before the prediction becomes
locked.

### What happens after the match?

After Goalstery has sufficient match-result information, your prediction
is evaluated.

Depending on the prediction type and result, it may be marked as
correct, wrong, or remain pending while the match is unresolved.

Correct predictions award Cups according to the scoring rules applicable
to that prediction.

### What happens if a match is postponed or cancelled?

If a match is postponed, cancelled, abandoned, suspended, or otherwise
does not have a result suitable for settlement, affected predictions may
remain pending.

They remain pending until Goalstery determines that sufficient
information exists to settle them or another resolution is appropriate.

### Can a settled prediction change later?

In unusual cases, yes.

Match-data providers or official competitions may correct previously
reported information. Technical or settlement errors can also occur.

When necessary to maintain accurate results and competition integrity,
Goalstery may correct a settlement and recalculate affected predictions,
Cups, rankings, or competition results.

## Cups

### What are Cups?

Cups are Goalstery's in-app scoring points.

You earn Cups through gameplay, including by making correct predictions.

Your Cups can be used to measure your performance and determine rankings
in Goalstery.

### Do different predictions award different amounts of Cups?

Yes.

Prediction types may have different scoring values depending on their
nature or difficulty.

The applicable reward should be shown as part of the prediction
experience before the prediction is submitted.

### Do Cups have monetary value?

No.

Cups cannot be purchased, sold, withdrawn, transferred to another user,
exchanged for money or cryptocurrency, or exchanged for goods or
services.

Cups are an in-app scoring mechanism only.

### Are Cups cryptocurrency?

No.

Cups are not cryptocurrency, tokens, money, stored value, or a financial
balance.

They exist only as part of Goalstery's gameplay and ranking system.

## Leaderboard

### How does the leaderboard work?

Goalstery leaderboards rank participating players according to the
applicable competition rules and their Goalstery results.

Depending on the screen or competition, Goalstery may show views such as
top-ranked players, players around your current position, or a larger
list of participants.

### Why can my leaderboard position change?

Your position can change when your predictions are settled, other
players' predictions are settled, pending matches receive results, a
settlement or scoring error is corrected, or competition data is
recalculated.

This means a leaderboard can continue changing while relevant
predictions remain unresolved.

### What happens if a result is corrected?

Goalstery may recalculate affected Cups and rankings.

If a correction affects a competition result, leaderboard positions and
winner determination may also change.

## Prize Cups

### Can I win a real prize?

Some Goalstery Cups may offer separately announced real-world prizes.

These may include cryptocurrency prizes such as USDT or GRAM.

Not every Cup has a real-world prize. If a Cup offers one, the prize and
applicable rules should be identified as part of that competition.

### Do I have to pay to enter a Prize Cup?

No.

Participation in Goalstery Prize Cups is free.

Goalstery does not require an entry fee in money, cryptocurrency,
Telegram Stars, Cups, or another item of value to participate.

### Are Cups converted into USDT or GRAM?

No.

Cups themselves have no monetary value and are never converted into
cryptocurrency.

A cryptocurrency prize is a separate prize offered for a particular
competition.

### Are Prize Cups available everywhere?

Not necessarily.

Goalstery is intended for international use, but a particular Prize Cup
may be unavailable to users in certain countries, territories, or
regions.

Goalstery may apply geographic or eligibility restrictions where
offering a competition or delivering a prize may be prohibited,
restricted, impractical, or subject to requirements Goalstery cannot
reasonably satisfy.

Availability of Goalstery does not guarantee eligibility for every Prize
Cup.

### How do I claim a cryptocurrency prize?

If you win an eligible cryptocurrency prize, you may be asked to contact
the official Goalstery Telegram support account.

You will then provide a compatible public cryptocurrency wallet address
so the prize can be sent manually.

Always check the wallet address and network carefully before providing
it.

Blockchain transactions may be irreversible.

### Will Goalstery ever ask for my seed phrase or private key?

No.

**Goalstery will never need your seed phrase, private key, wallet
password, or similar secret credentials to send you a prize.**

To receive a cryptocurrency payment, Goalstery only needs the
appropriate public wallet address and any information reasonably
required to verify the prize claim.

Never send your seed phrase or private key to anyone claiming to
represent Goalstery.

## Account

### How is my Goalstery account created?

Goalstery operates as a Telegram Mini App.

Your Telegram account is used to authenticate and identify you, so you
do not need to create a separate Goalstery password.

### What Telegram information does Goalstery receive?

Goalstery may receive information made available through the Telegram
Mini App interface that is necessary to identify you and provide the
Service.

This may include your Telegram user ID, username, display name, profile
photo, language, and similar profile information made available through
Telegram.

Goalstery does not request access to your Telegram messages, contacts,
or phone number as part of normal use.

### Can I use multiple accounts?

A Telegram account corresponds to a Goalstery account.

Using multiple accounts to gain an unfair advantage, manipulate
competitions or rankings, circumvent restrictions, or otherwise abuse
Goalstery is prohibited.

### How do I delete my Goalstery account?

Contact the official Goalstery Telegram support account and request
account deletion.

Goalstery will process the request and delete or anonymize associated
personal information as appropriate.

Some historical competition information may be retained in anonymized
form when necessary to preserve the integrity of completed competitions
and leaderboards.

## Fair Play

### Can I use statistics or AI to help make predictions?

Yes.

You may use football statistics, research, mathematical models,
artificial intelligence, or other analytical tools to help decide which
prediction you want to make.

### What is not allowed?

Goalstery does not permit unfair or abusive interaction with the
Service.

You must not use bots to automatically interact with Goalstery or submit
predictions; exploit bugs or unintended application behavior; manipulate
Cups, rankings, competition results, or leaderboards; use multiple
accounts to gain an unfair advantage; circumvent access controls, rate
limits, bans, or eligibility restrictions; interfere with Goalstery's
APIs, servers, infrastructure, or security; falsify information used to
determine Prize Cup eligibility; or engage in fraud or similar abuse.

### What can happen if I break the rules?

Depending on the violation, Goalstery may correct affected results,
invalidate predictions, adjust Cups or rankings, remove eligibility for
a competition or prize, or permanently block the account.

## Match Data

### Where does Goalstery get football data?

Goalstery may obtain fixtures, scores, match status, and other football
information from third-party sports-data sources, including
football-data.org.

Third-party sports data can sometimes be delayed, incomplete, or
corrected after publication.

### Which result does Goalstery use for scoring?

Goalstery makes the final determination of the match information used
for its own prediction settlement and competitions.

When necessary, Goalstery may consider official competition information,
sports-data providers, and other reliable sources.

For full details, see the Goalstery Privacy Policy.

Goalstery may display advertising.

If third-party advertising services are introduced, those services may
have their own privacy practices.

The Goalstery Privacy Policy will describe Goalstery's applicable data

Keep your Telegram account secure and do not give other people access to
it.

For cryptocurrency prizes, never share seed phrases, private keys,
wallet passwords, or authentication secrets.

Goalstery does not require these secrets to deliver a prize.

## Troubleshooting

### My prediction is still pending

A prediction can remain pending while Goalstery waits for sufficient
match-result information.

This may happen particularly when a match is postponed, suspended,
abandoned, cancelled, or otherwise unresolved.

If you believe a completed match has remained pending incorrectly,
contact Goalstery Support.

### My Cups or ranking changed

Cups and rankings can change as predictions are settled and other
players receive their results.

They may also change if Goalstery corrects inaccurate match data,
settlement information, scoring, or competition results.

### I think my prediction was settled incorrectly

Contact Goalstery Support and provide enough information to identify the
match and prediction.

Goalstery can review the settlement and correct it where appropriate.

### I cannot access a Prize Cup

A particular Prize Cup may have eligibility or geographic restrictions.

Access to Goalstery itself does not guarantee access to every Prize Cup.

If you believe you should be eligible, contact Goalstery Support.
